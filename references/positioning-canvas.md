## Quality checks

- **Who** can be observed in a chat, a post, or a purchase; not just a demographic.
- **Substitute** includes the cheapest commodity and "do nothing".
- **Gap** is a decision-changing constraint, not a feature gap.
- **What** is one promise, not a product description, not a slogan.
- **How** gives the initiator a sentence or action and the companion a reason to come/return.
- **Proof** is evidence, not adjectives.
- **Kill criteria** are written before research, not after.

## Course mode mapping

When used for the 《品牌策略与设计管理》 course:

| Canvas | Course field |

| Who | 3 specific tags + 3 pains |
| What | 2W1H What, <=15 Chinese characters |
| How | 2W1H How, one communication method |

| Gap + proof | 3 competitors + SWOT |

Course requirements: A p27 (persona, 2W1H, honeycomb, name + slogan), A p20 (Who/What/How details), A p36/p53 (competitors + SWOT), B p47 (week schedule). Page count and pitch length are packaging choices unless the instructor says otherwise.

## Example (shade project)

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Scene | Weekend midday, city park, two people sitting and eating |
| Who — behaviour | Invites a friend, chooses the place, packs two-person supplies |
| Relationship | Close friend, not stranger-buddy, not family |
| Initiator | The one who sends the message and plans |
| Companion | The one who decides whether to come again |
| Existing substitute | Find shade / change time / bring umbrella / cancel |
| Constraint | Park bans pegs and ropes; tents are stuffy; shade is taken |
| Gap | No pegs, no ropes, two-person seated shade |
| What | 两个人吃饭的那片荫 |
| How | A transferable invitation card: time, place, who brings what |
| Proof | Rule text, three competitor prices, journey weakest first minute |
| Kill | No decision-changing shade constraint, or invitation never sent |
