# Question Bank

Use one cluster at a time. Do not dump the whole bank on the user.

## Claim

- What is the one sentence you want me to believe?
- Who is the "who" in that sentence, as a behaviour?
- What is the existing substitute, including doing nothing?
- What does the substitute cost in time, money, weight, or social friction?
- What mechanism does your concept use?
- What metric would move, and by how much?
- What result would prove you wrong?

## Audience and relationship

- Is the real filter the route, the behaviour, the occasion, or the relationship?
- How often does this happen? Below once a month, is a dedicated product justified?
- Is "two people" a real unit or a design convenience?
- Who initiates, who carries, who pays, who cleans?
- Is the companion a validator or a co-user? What makes them return?
- Are you using stranger-buddy evidence for a close-friend occasion?
- Are you using family evidence for a two-friend occasion?
- What would the low-effort version of this person do?

## Substitute and current behaviour

- What do they actually use now?
- What does it cost and weigh?
- Is the pain leakage, smell, time, bulk, social awkwardness, or none of these?
- Are your words the user's words, or your design language?
- Does a disposable, takeout, borrow, or "just find shade" option make the problem disappear?
- What did they change last time because of the constraint?

## Rules and constraints

- What is the official rule text, date, and jurisdiction?
- Does the rule ban the object, a feature, or a behaviour?
- Is the enforcement consistent, or site-by-site?
- What is the narrower allowed version?
- Did the user actually change a decision because of the rule, or only mention it?

## Weight, volume, cost, feasibility

- What does the concept replace, and what is the net difference?
- What is the empty weight, loaded weight, and one-hand carry distance?
- Does it fit transit, stairs, a small apartment, or a shared hallway?
- Can it be cleaned in the time users will actually spend?
- Does it have a dead corner, a leak path, or a failure point?
- What is the target price, and what does the nearest substitute cost?

## Evidence and bias

- Are the users friends, design students, or people who already share your hypothesis?
- Did you ask about the last real occasion, or a hypothetical purchase?
- Did you record negative cases?
- Did you mistake "I hate this" for "I will pay for this"?
- Did you test a prototype or only a rendering?
- Is the sample big enough to say anything beyond "these few people"?
- Is the quote verbatim or a paraphrase?

## Brand and promise

- What is the one promise?
- Is it a promise or a product description?
- Is the slogan doing the What's job?
- What is the brand's role in the relationship: host, helper, excuse, gift, ritual?
- If the logo were removed, what would still be recognisable?
- What does the brand refuse to do?

## Deck

- What question does each page answer?
- Which page is a product feature without a user behaviour?
- Which page is an adjective?
- Where is the weakest touchpoint?
- Which claim is still `[assumption]`?
- What would a teacher or investor ask first?

## Abandonment

- What result would make you kill this?
- If the commodity baseline wins, will you actually stop?
- If the handoff or behaviour never happens, what is the next direction?
- What reusable insight would you keep from the kill?
